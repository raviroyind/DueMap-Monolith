How auto-link works

When a QuickBooks or Xero customer has exactly one active lease, DueMap links them automatically during sync. When the relationship is ambiguous (multiple leases per customer, or a lease that was created before the sync), the lease lands here for you to map manually. Linked leases route notices to the customer's email and phone; unmapped leases won't receive notices until they're linked.

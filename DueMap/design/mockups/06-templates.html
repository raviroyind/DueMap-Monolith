How template resolution works

At send time, DueMap picks the most specific template for each notice: first a state-specific override matching the lease's state, then your generic PM template, then the built-in system default. Required legal placeholders (tenant_name, amount_owed, due_date) are enforced at every layer — you can rewrite the wording but cannot drop them.
